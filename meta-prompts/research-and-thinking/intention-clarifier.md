<task>
Create a new skill for my AI coding agent called "intention-clarifier", stored
wherever my harness loads skills from.

The skill's job: take a single vague intention, nagging thought, or half-formed idea
and surface what I actually want, ending on one concrete first step I can take within
24 hours.

The skill must include: (1) trigger conditions — a vague desire, "help me figure out",
"I'm not sure what I want", "something's been on my mind", or when I seem stuck or
overwhelmed — and an explicit non-trigger: do not use it when the goal is already clear
and only the plan is missing, do not use it to stress-test an existing plan (grilling
owns that), and do not use it for a multi-topic brain dump (brain-dump-processor owns
that); (2) a reflection-back phase that restates the core desire, the underlying
tension, and two or three candidate goals before any question is asked; (3) a targeted
question bank grouped WHAT / WHY / HOW / CONSTRAINTS, asked one at a time and only the
few that fit — never all of them; (4) a fixed output: the real goal in one sentence, why
this and why now, what success looks like, what is actually in the way, the one concrete
24-hour first step, and what that step unlocks; (5) a routing step that names the honest
next move — ready to act, needs breakdown (grilling or direct decomposition), needs
delegation (goal-prompt-generator), learning-shaped (teach), needs more thinking, or drop
it; (6) an "if still fuzzy" escalation with meta-questions, including the possibility that
the fuzziness is protecting me from something and should be named rather than forced.

Write it to the open-skills authoring standard: six elements (frontmatter, trigger
description, numbered steps, a "Never..." guardrail, an output contract, a verification
standard), prose-heavy, no inline bold, no AI-isms.
</task>
